7. Duplicates — the real root cause
------------------------------------
    "Loot : Nutriburst Collagen powder 299 (70% off)"
    "Grab : Nutriburst Collagen powder @ 299 70% off"
    "Grab ₹298 (10KG Detergent Powder)"  /  "Loot : 10KG Detergent Powder @298"

Three separate bugs in the product signature made these sign differently:
  a) the banner word ("Loot :", "Grab ") was read as the BRAND. It is stripped
     now, colon or no colon, using the same _SAFE_PREFIX_RE the safe rewrite
     uses (so the two can never drift apart).
  b) a price written without "₹" ("@298", "at 167", "299 (70% off)") was read
     as a MODEL NUMBER. _SIG_AMOUNT_RE now reads every spelling, and a bare
     number equal to the line's own price is never a model.
  c) a product with no model number at all needed FOUR agreeing words, so
     "Nutriburst Collagen powder" was un-dedupable. Three long words is enough.

Guarded in the other direction (a signature that matches too eagerly SKIPS a
real deal), with a regression test covering:
  Airdopes 141 vs 131, S23 128GB vs 256GB, 5 Burner vs 3 Burner,
  Collagen vs Biotin, and Nivea MEN vs WOMEN — the last one was a PRE-EXISTING
  collision (gender words are now variant words, like "pro"/"fe").

ops/quality_audit.py mirrors this block; it was re-synced with
`python3 ops/sync_identity.py` and the suite enforces that they match.

Round 4 — "review ayyaka okka sari approve vasthe chalu kada?"
=================================================================

Half right, and the other half is the dangerous half. Read this one.

TRUE: Amazon reviews the channel you SUBMIT. They do not go hunting through
your other Telegram channels at application time, and the review copy this bot
now renders (name + price + link, no loot/urgency/claims, no t.me promo) is
built for exactly that review.

NOT TRUE: "approve vachaka chalu, migatha channels lo em avvadu."

Amazon's Operating Agreement (affiliate-program.amazon.in, "top do's and
don'ts", section "Sites and social channels"):

  "Do keep your websites, social media channels, and mobile applications ...
   that you use to drive traffic to Amazon updated in your account on
   Associates Central."

and Amazon states plainly that listing a channel wrongly / not listing a
channel that carries your links "can lead to closure of your account".

So the rule is not "one channel gets reviewed once". The rule is:
    wherever your TAGGED link appears, that channel must be declared.
Approval is not a one-time gate - compliance is ongoing, and Amazon audits link
placement against your declared properties.

THE CONCRETE RISK HERE
----------------------
The moment you set AMAZON_TAG (which you must, to earn anything), the SAME
tagged Amazon link was going to be published to ALL owned channels -
LootZoneIndia11, SecretLootIndia1, PowerLoots1, Under99Deals11, ... - because
the tag is applied in the render path, before fan-out. Those channels are
undeclared AND full of "Loot"/urgency wording. That is the account-closure
scenario, and it would have happened silently on the first tagged post.

THE FIX (this round)
--------------------
    AMAZON_TAG_TARGETS   (default: smartbuyhub11)

Our Associates tag now rides ONLY on the channels named there. On every other
channel, strip_amazon_tag_for_undeclared() removes our `tag` parameter at
delivery time - per target, after the stored copy is final.

  * the deal still posts everywhere, with a working link (nothing is lost)
  * only OUR tag is removed, and only the `tag` parameter
  * non-Amazon links are untouched; EarnKaro links are unaffected
  * a foreign tag is not ours to protect and is left to the existing gates

If/when you declare more channels in Associates Central, add them:
    AMAZON_TAG_TARGETS=smartbuyhub11,LootZoneIndia11

WHAT YOU STILL HAVE TO DO YOURSELF
----------------------------------
1. Declare @smartbuyhub11 in Associates Central as the FULL URL
   (https://t.me/smartbuyhub11 — not "t.me", not "telegram.org").
2. Put the affiliate disclosure in the channel bio/description, e.g.
   "As an Amazon Associate I earn from qualifying purchases."
   Amazon requires it at channel level; the bot cannot write your bio.
3. Keep the channel PUBLIC (no private/invite-only), posts visible to
   non-subscribers.
4. Do not put "Amazon" in the channel name/handle (trademark rule).
5. Make 3 qualifying sales in 180 days or the account is closed anyway.
6. Set AMAZON_TAG only after approval, and leave AMAZON_TAG_TARGETS at the
   declared channel(s).

NOTE ON TELEGRAM ITSELF: Amazon's approved-social list names Facebook,
Instagram, X, YouTube, TikTok and Twitch. Telegram is not on that list - it is
a grey area. People do run approved Telegram channels, but understand you are
not standing on the same firm ground as a website, and keep the channel clean.

WHY THE APPROACH CHANGED
------------------------
Until now the review copy was a BLOCKLIST: remove "loot", remove "hurry",

thought of is the one the reviewer reads. Real source posts carry "PRICE ERROR",

      2. a product line - 2+ real words, no banned word

    everything else is dropped.

Nothing is invented: every word and every digit still comes from the source,
and the price is kept EXACTLY as the source printed it (₹298 / at 258 / @167).
A price we re-format is a price we could get wrong, and a wrong price is the one
thing a marketplace programme does not forgive.

    SAFE_STRICT_REBUILD=true   (default; set false to fall back to the blocklist)

Round 6 — daily 20-30 Amazon posts on the review channel
=================================================================

"daily 20-30 post cheyali, Amazon vi veyu, reject cheyakunda"

Three rejection risks were still open. All three are now enforced in code, so
they cannot be forgotten on a busy day.

1. LINK-LEVEL DISCLOSURE  (the biggest one)
-------------------------------------------
Amazon/FTC require a clear disclosure NEXT TO the link on EVERY post. The
channel bio alone is NOT enough - Amazon's own help page says the link-level
disclosure must be "clear" and "conspicuous", and names "(paid link)", "#ad",
"#CommissionsEarned" as acceptable. Missing it is one of the most common
rejection/warning reasons.

    SHOPPING_DISCLOSURE="#ad (paid link)"    (default)

add_link_disclosure() appends it to every review-channel post, at delivery,
AFTER every cleaning pass - so no later pass can strip it - and never twice.

    Boat Airdopes 141 at 899
    https://www.amazon.in/dp/B0X

    #ad (paid link)

2. AMAZON ONLY
--------------
The channel is submitted for the AMAZON programme, so a Flipkart/Myntra link on
it is off-programme noise to a reviewer.

    SHOPPING_AMAZON_ONLY=true   (default)

Only a post whose links are ALL Amazon reaches this channel. A mixed post
(Amazon + Flipkart) is excluded too - otherwise the off-programme link rides
along. Everything else keeps going to the other channels exactly as today.
Enforced twice: at routing AND at delivery.

3. A DAILY CAP
--------------
20-30 curated posts/day reads like a shop. 200 reads like spam and is itself a
rejection risk.

    SHOPPING_DAILY_CAP=30       (default; 0 = unlimited)

Counted per IST day in a `shopping_quota` table, so the pace SURVIVES RESTARTS
(an in-memory counter would reset to zero every deploy and quietly blow the
cap). Old rows are pruned after 3 days.

Over the cap, the post is closed for this channel only ("daily cap reached") -
every other channel still receives it.

TUNING FOR YOUR 20-30 TARGET
----------------------------
Default is 30/day. For a tighter 20:
    SHOPPING_DAILY_CAP=20
The channel takes the deals as they come; if your Amazon flow exceeds the cap,
the surplus simply goes to the other channels.

WHAT A REVIEWER NOW SEES, EVERY POST
------------------------------------
    <product name> <price exactly as the source printed it>
    <Amazon link>

    #ad (paid link)

No loot/urgency wording, no "buy max quantity", no unverifiable claims, no
discount percentages, no MRP, no emoji shouting, no t.me promo, no trick/glitch
posts, no non-Amazon links, no duplicates, and never more than the daily cap.

STILL YOURS TO DO (cannot be done from code)

  * channel PUBLIC, posts visible to non-subscribers

Round 8 — the duplicate hole in ROUNDUP posts
=================================================================

USER RULE (2026-09-06): "anni vere channels lo anni multiple times
rakudna and duplicates lekunda".

Audit of the four dedup layers found three of them sound and one with a
real hole:

  1. seen_message()          - same source message twice          OK
  2. content_deal_key()      - same text from any source, 24h     OK
  3. deal_claims/posted_deals- two live jobs racing               OK
  4. product_signature()     - same PRODUCT per channel, 24h      HOLE

product_signature() returned None for any post with 3+ merchant links,
i.e. every roundup/list post. No key means the per-channel repeat guard
never ran on them, so the same list arriving again from a second source
- different shortener, different banner, items shuffled - was published
a second time. That is the "multiple times" the user reported.

_roundup_signature() now keys a roundup on the SET of products it names,
using the same _product_identity() the single-product path uses:

  * order-independent  (sources shuffle the items)
  * link-independent   (two shorteners, one product)
  * banner-independent (_SAFE_PREFIX_RE strips "Loot :" / "Grab :")
  * 2-item floor       (one match is too thin to drop a live deal on)

A list that ADDS or DROPS an item is a different post and still goes
out, and the existing "better copy" rule is untouched, so a genuine
price drop is never swallowed.

Verified: A and a reordered A from another source now collide; A plus a
fourth product does not; single-product posts keep their SIG: scheme.
