THE THREE DEFECTS (found by reading the delivery path against that report)
-------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. THE LONG LINKS (the "asal long links velthunnai" half).
   Delivery rewrites OUR short links for channels not declared to Amazon
   (strip_amazon_tag_for_undeclared): the bit.ly must not hide our Associates
   tag from an undeclared channel. But the swap went back to the FULL
   destination URL - fine for a /dp/ link (~33 chars) and DISASTROUS for an
   Amazon hidden-keywords/SEARCH link, which exploded to 300+ characters on
   every loot channel (and from there into the WhatsApp channels via the
   bridge). The swap is now the UNTAGGED COMPACT form (tag + btn_ref/qid/psc
   session junk gone, /dp/ collapsed), and anything still long is shrunk by a
   new delivery-time pass.

2. THE LOST COMMISSION (the "earnkaro tho short ga cheyatledu" half).
   compact_flipkart_product_link() rebuilt a generated Flipkart link keeping
   `pid` ONLY - it dropped affExtParam1/affExtParam2/affid/subid, i.e. the
   EarnKaro publisher id that makes the click pay US. A tidy link that earns
   nothing. Compaction now keeps the attribution keys (FLIPKART_ATTRIBUTION_
   QUERY_KEYS) and still drops the session noise (lid/marketplace/srno/otracker).

3. NO SECOND CHANCE AT DELIVERY.
   The render-time shorten pass could miss (Bitly busy/rate-limited exactly
   when a long search link needed it) and the raw long link sailed out. New
   AffiliateClient.shorten_leftover_long_links() runs on the way to every
   channel except the review channel (native links there are the standing
   rule): anything still over SHORTEN_MIN_LEN is shortened NOW. The URL is
   shortened exactly as it stands (our tag and the EarnKaro attribution ride
   behind the hop and stay visible to the provenance gates), already-short
   links and every shortener domain are never re-wrapped (no Bitly quota on an
   ekaro.in link), and if the shortener is down the link is kept - a deal is
   never lost, only made neat.

WHAT THE BOT DOES NOW (pinned by the suites, all green)
  * An undeclared channel never meets a raw long Amazon URL: the swap is the
    clean untagged form and the second-chance pass puts it behind a short link
    (the shortener is even asked with the UNTAGGED destination).
  * A generated Flipkart/EarnKaro link keeps affExtParam2=<5478322> through
    compaction - tidy AND monetized.
  * Delivery-time: no link over the threshold leaves for a channel while a
    shortener works. Review channel (smartbuyhub11) stays native by rule.
  * A dead shortener keeps the link (and with it the deal) exactly as it is.
