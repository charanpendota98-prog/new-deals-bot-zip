HYPD SCOPE — MEESHO ONLY, OUR STORE 93944 / smartdeals
======================================================

USER SCOPE
  Use our HYPD account only for Meesho. Shopsy is explicitly excluded.

CONFIGURATION
  HYPD_STORE_ID=93944
  HYPD_STORE_SLUG=smartdeals
  HYPD_ALWAYS_BITLY=true
  HYPD_MERCHANT_DOMAINS=meesho.com

The bot pins its allowed HYPD merchant to Meesho even if an older .env still
contains `shopsy.in`. HYPD links must belong to our store and resolve to a
verified Meesho product page before they can be learned or posted. Old learned
Shopsy mappings are ignored and removed when encountered.

WHY THE DESTINATION CHECK MATTERS
  The HYPD share URL carries the creator attribution. The bot must keep that
  URL intact for Meesho, but must not publish a Shopsy/other-store HYPD URL just
  because it came from our HYPD store ID. Unknown destinations fail closed and
  are not stored as Meesho links.

HOW TO LEARN A MEESHO LINK
  HYPD has no public link-creation API. Create the product link in the HYPD app,
  then teach the bot the returned share URL. Run this on the bot/server checkout
  with internet access:

    python3 ops/hypd_links.py 'https://hypd.store/93944/afflink/<actual-token>'

  The tool prints the destination. It stores the mapping only if the URL is
  ours and the merchant destination is on Meesho. Shopsy, other merchants, and
  unverified destinations are rejected, not silently learned.

CHECKS
  python3 ops/hypd_links.py --list
      List only learned Meesho mappings.

  python3 ops/hypd_links.py --wanted
      Show Meesho products that need an HYPD link.

  python3 ops/hypd_links.py --lookup 'https://www.meesho.com/<actual-product-url>'
      Check whether a Meesho product already has our HYPD mapping.

  python3 ops/hypd_links.py --dry-run 'https://hypd.store/93944/afflink/<actual-token>'
      Verify a candidate without storing it.

  python3 ops/earnkaro_check.py --hypd-only
      Live-check the configured candidate links: it verifies a Meesho destination
      before shortening and checks that Bitly returns to our HYPD store.

BEHAVIOUR
  * Bare Meesho product + matching verified mapping: publish our HYPD link,
    shortened with Bitly when available (the raw HYPD link is the outage fallback).
  * Bare Meesho product + no mapping: log HYPD MISSING / HYPD WANTED and keep the
    product visible as an explicitly unmonetized merchant link.
  * Shopsy: never route it through HYPD; use the ordinary affiliate path or a
    clean merchant fallback.
  * HYPD share with an unknown destination: do not publish it as a Meesho link.
  * The WhatsApp direct-source bridge applies the same check before retaining
    or shortening a raw HYPD share; Shopsy is replaced with a clean URL, while
    foreign-store/unknown HYPD links are removed.
