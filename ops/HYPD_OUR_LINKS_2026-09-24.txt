WHY IT WAS BROKEN
  A hypd.store afflink is a FINAL, MONETIZED link: the store, the token and
  HYPD's attribution (affid=infhypd, affExtParam1=<our HYPD account>,
  affExtParam2=<token>) live in it. The pipeline used to
    * resolve() the redirect, then
    * strip those parameters as "tracking noise",
  so the post went out as a clean, UNTAGGED merchant page that pays nothing —
  the opposite of what the user asked for.

WHAT THE BOT DOES NOW (pinned by test_hypd_links.py, 72 checks, all green)
  * OUR hypd.store/<store>/afflink/<token> link is treated as OUR link:
    never unwrapped, never parameter-stripped, never sent to EarnKaro.
  * It is ALWAYS Bitly-shortened (HYPD_ALWAYS_BITLY=true) — even though the
    URL is short — exactly as the user asked ("bitly tho shorten ga chesi").
  * If Bitly/is.gd is down the RAW HYPD link is posted instead: the commission
    link is never lost, the post is never left unmonetized.
  * The merchant page behind each link is recorded (hypd_links table) and the
    product identity is taken from it, so the same product is still deduped.
  * A BARE Meesho/Shopsy link (no EarnKaro campaign — EarnKaro answers
    "no campaign" for the whole Meesho family) now earns on OUR HYPD link when
    we already have one for that product, instead of posting untagged.
  * Another creator's hypd.store link (store != 93944/smartdeals) is NEVER
    published as ours — it would pay them.
